# Missions

The board is 60" × 44". Red deploys bottom/left, Green top/right. Every unit starts in reserves and deploys when it is first activated.

**Vault Breaker** is the official 40k Reforged mission. The other four are this project's own adaptations of classic mission ideas, rewritten for Reforged's alternating activations. They are not official Reforged rules.

## Shared rules for the VP missions

- **Control:** a side controls an objective if it has more models within 4" of its centre than the other side. A tie, or nobody there, means no one controls it.
- **Scoring:** happens at the end of each battle round. Reforged has no player turns, so both sides score at the same time. The app shows a scoring card when a round ends.
- **Length:** 5 rounds. Most VP wins, and equal VP is a draw. Wiping out the enemy army wins at once.

## Overview

| Mission | Deployment | Scoring |
|---|---|---|
| Vault Breaker | Diagonal corners (official) | Relics. Three units with reforged relics on the battlefield wins. No round limit. |
| Take and Hold | Dawn of War: long edges, 12" deep | 3 VP per objective controlled, from round 2. |
| Purge the Foe | Search and Destroy: table quarters, 9" clear of centre | 4 VP for at least one kill that round, +4 for more kills than the opponent. From round 2 also: 4 VP for controlling at least one objective, +4 for controlling more. |
| Supply Drop | Hammer and Anvil: short edges, 18" deep | Only the 3 no-man's-land objectives score: 5 VP in rounds 2–3, 8 VP in round 4, 15 VP in round 5. A random one is removed after scoring rounds 3 and 4. |
| Scorched Earth | Crucible of Battle: diagonal zones on the short edges | 3 VP per objective controlled, from round 2. Units can burn objectives for +5 VP, or +10 VP for the enemy home objective. |

## Vault Breaker (official)

- **Setup:**
  - Deployment zones are 24" × 24" triangles in opposite corners.
  - Three vaults run diagonally across the table: one in the centre, the other two 16" from a short edge and 8" from a long edge.
  - Each zone's diagonal edge has two forges, 8" in from each end. The forges in Green's zone belong to Red, and vice versa.
- **Vault:**
  - After your active unit's End step, it captures a vault if it is within 4" and has more models than all enemy units within 4" combined.
  - Roll 2D6 and take a relic matching either die. Doubles make the capturing unit ready again.
- **Forge:**
  - A unit with a relic that captures one of your forges reforges the relic, and the unit becomes ready again.
  - Each forge can be captured once per round, and not by a unit that was in reserves this round.
- **Win:** three different units with reforged relics on the battlefield. You also win by tabling your opponent or if they surrender.

## Take and Hold

- **Setup:** zones run 12" deep along the long edges. There are 5 objectives:
  - one in the centre;
  - a home objective in each zone, 6" from the edge;
  - two on the centre line, 10" from each short edge.
- **Scoring:** from round 2, 3 VP per objective controlled (up to 15 VP a round).

## Purge the Foe

- **Setup:**
  - Red deploys in the bottom-left quarter, Green in the top-right. Nobody deploys within 9" of the centre.
  - Objectives: the centre, a home objective in each deployment quarter, and one in each empty quarter.
- **Scoring:**
  - Every round: 4 VP for at least one kill, +4 VP for more kills than the opponent.
  - From round 2: 4 VP for controlling at least one objective, +4 VP for controlling more.
- **Note:** mark destroyed units in the app straight away so kills count in the right round.

## Supply Drop

- **Setup:**
  - Zones run 18" deep along the short edges.
  - Markers: centre, North and South (on the centre line, 8" from the long edges), and a home marker in each zone.
  - Only Centre, North and South score.
- **Scoring:** 5 VP each in rounds 2 and 3, 8 VP each in round 4, and 15 VP for the last one in round 5.
- **Special:** the app removes one supply objective at random after scoring round 3, and another after scoring round 4.

## Scorched Earth

- **Setup:**
  - The zones are large triangles on opposite short edges.
  - Objectives: the centre, a home objective deep in each zone, and one in each empty corner of no man's land.
- **Scoring:** from round 2, 3 VP per objective controlled.
- **Burn:**
  - From round 2, in a unit's End step, a unit can burn an objective outside its own deployment zone if it:
    - made no attacks this turn;
    - is within 4" of the objective;
    - has more models than the enemy within 4".
  - Burning scores +5 VP, or +10 VP for the enemy home objective. The objective is removed for the rest of the battle.
  - Each unit can burn at most one objective per activation.

Objective positions in the app are approximate. Set them up by eye from the map.
